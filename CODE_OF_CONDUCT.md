# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in MorphoQuant AI respectful, constructive, and accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while remaining rigorous about scientific and technical claims.
- Critique ideas, methods, and code rather than attacking people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Respect privacy, research ethics, licensing, and data-use restrictions.
- Acknowledge contributions and cite sources appropriately.
- Welcome questions and constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, doxxing, threats, spam, and publication of confidential or identifiable research data without authorization are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports should be made privately through the repository's contact or security-reporting channels.

This code applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces used to represent the project.
